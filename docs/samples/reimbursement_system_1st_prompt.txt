A logistics company needs an employee expense reimbursement web application. Field drivers must upload receipt photos from mobile browsers, with automated OCR extraction of merchant name, date, and total amount. Employees group expenses into monthly reports and submit them for manager approval. Department managers need a review dashboard to approve, reject, or flag items exceeding company policy limits, triggering email and Slack notifications. Approved reports must export to CSV and sync with QuickBooks Online via REST API. Role-based access control for employees, managers, and finance admins is required, with Docker deployment on AWS and a target timeline of 12 weeks.
